# Platform & Publishing (Stage 7)

## Platform calibration (observed tendencies, not guarantees)
- **Instagram Reels:** slightly more polish works; saves and DM shares are strong signals.
- **TikTok:** rawer, spontaneous delivery works; comments and duets are strong signals; assume a cold audience.
- **YouTube Shorts:** the first second competes with the next Short; a clearly flagged open loop is tolerated.
- **Unknown platform:** assume a cold audience, muted playback, and high first-second competition.

## Publishing package (`publish`)
- **Caption that adds:** it does a job the video can't. Use one of three: **the steps written out** (numbered, copyable), **a resource** (the template, the exact setting, the link), or **a question that earns comments** (one with a real answer, e.g. "what's the first line you'd put in yours?"). It is **never a transcript**: `caption_echo` warns when it shares 60% of the script's key words. The first line works alone (only about 125 characters show before "more"); then 2–4 short lines; then the CTA. Written in the creator's voice, never generic hype. Examples: `craft-standards.md` §8.
- **Search keywords:** 3–5 phrases the target viewer would type, woven naturally into the caption and on-screen text.
- **Hashtags:** 3–5, specific to the niche and the audience (e.g. a tag the target viewer follows), never mega-tags like #reels or #viral.
- **Cover text:** 2–4 words.
- **Pinned comment** and the first reply.
- **Keyword automation** (if the CTA uses a comment word): the keyword plus spelling variants, 3–5 rotating public replies, and the DM text. Remind the creator to test from a second account and to check that it fires on ad comments too.
- **Hook test plan** (`test_plan`): pick the two hooks with the most *different* mechanisms and suggest posting them as separate tests (or with the platform's trial/test feature if the creator's account has it). Say what to compare (3-second hold, average watch time, saves). **Never predict which one wins.**
- **AI label** (`ai_label`): `true` whenever the voice or photorealistic footage is AI-generated.
- **Captions:** the dashboard exports an `.srt` file from the final script's timings. Remind the creator to check line breaks after the edit.

## Guideline check (flag anything relevant)
- **Originality:** no reposted clips or other apps' watermarks. Re-shoot a winning idea; don't re-upload it.
- **Audio rights:** use the platform's licensed audio; business accounts may have a limited music library.
- **Paid partnerships:** sponsored, gifted, or affiliate content uses the platform's disclosure.
- **AI media:** a cloned voice or photorealistic AI footage needs the AI label.
- **Regulated claims:** health, finance, legal, or guaranteed results. These are blocked upstream by the Trust Officer (`claims-trust.md`); here, add the required disclosure.
- **Engagement bait** and misleading covers.
- **Accessibility:** captions on.
